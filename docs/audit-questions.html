Appendix A — Audit Question Finder

Last updated

23 October 2026

Start here when you have an audit question and want to know where the book answers it. The questions are grouped by audit area. Each row names the technique and the section that shows it at work. Links with a small arrow leave this book. Here they open the companion book, Applied Audit Analytics with R, where the technique is applied to a whole audit area.

Type a word in the box to narrow the list, for example duplicate, vendor, pension or map.
